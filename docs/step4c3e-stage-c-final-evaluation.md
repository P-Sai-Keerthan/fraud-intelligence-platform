## 10. Final recommendation

**`v2_dnn_lstm` seed 14 is eligible for a controlled-promotion decision.
`v2_dnn_only` seed 14 is not, under the approved alert gate. No promotion was
made, and none should follow from this report automatically.**

Points for the decision:

1. **Strength of the result.** The gates were fixed in advance and the data
   was untouched. Recall is 0.558 on the final hold-out, 0.556 on the
   development data and 0.573 on the earlier hold-out: the same model gives
   the same result on three independent sets of data.
2. **The alert margin is thin.** The upper bound is 9.83 against 10.0.
   Across the ten final datasets with full history, this artifact is above
   10 in one. The budget is met here; it is not guaranteed on other data.
3. **First-fraud detection.** The DNN-only artifact is better on it by
   0.086. If catching the first fraud matters more than total fraud caught,
   that is a reason to look again at the alert gate for the simpler model.
   That would be a new decision under a new protocol, not a reading of this
   one.
4. **This is one trained model.** Seed 14 sits at the upper end of the five
   LSTM training runs. Retraining would not necessarily reproduce it. A
   promotion would have to deploy these exact files.
5. **New customers.** Early transactions run at about 25 legitimate alerts
   per 1,000 under the current behaviour. A new-customer policy is still
   needed.
