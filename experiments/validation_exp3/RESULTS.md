## Reproducibility

* Bootstrap: 2000 replicates, seed 9101, unit: customer component (ring/household whole), within seed, pooled.
* Software: python 3.13.16, numpy 2.4.4, pandas 3.0.2, scikit_learn 1.8.0, tensorflow 2.21.0.
* Re-trained baselines: random forest and logistic regression fitted on 58,840 training rows (291 fraud) of the seed-42 v2 dataset (SHA-256 `a09d06114ce7c0dec4b93ff9946ab9506f5c7d25317bb95396b596f2f7b1ef08`).
* Per-row scores (about 54 MB) are not committed; their SHA-256 over the decompressed CSV content are in each results file (`meta.scores_content_sha256`) and `scripts` regenerate them deterministically (verified by a second full run).
